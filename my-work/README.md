## Practical Challenges in Reserve Risk

Kevin Chan and Michael Ramyar. GIRO 2015 (IFoA), plenary session "The Bigger Picture", 26 October 2015.

- Paper: [2015-chan-ramyar-practical-challenges-in-reserve-risk-paper.pdf](2015-chan-ramyar-practical-challenges-in-reserve-risk-paper.pdf) (this version dated October 2016). Source: [IFoA VLE](https://vle.actuaries.org.uk/course/info.php?id=1991); [IFoA website PDF](https://www.actuaries.org.uk/system/files/field/document/Plenary%203%20-%20Practical%20Challenges%20in%20Reserve%20Risk_0.pdf)
- Slides: [2015-chan-ramyar-practical-challenges-in-reserve-risk-slides.pdf](2015-chan-ramyar-practical-challenges-in-reserve-risk-slides.pdf) (version without animation). Source: [IFoA VLE](https://vle.actuaries.org.uk/pluginfile.php/143712/mod_resource/content/1/Plenary%203%20-%20A%20Bigger%20Picture%20FINAL%20%28wo%20animation%29.pdf)
- Keywords: reserving, reserve risk, stochastic reserving, bootstrap, Mack's model

**Abstract.**

> This document describes the challenges experienced in quantifying reserve risk with practical solutions suggested. It provides a pragmatic development towards advancing the assessment of reserve risk. It is aimed at general insurance actuaries who want an understanding of the challenges encountered in reserve risk without becoming too engaged in the technical details which can often be a distraction to the underlying issues. However, owing to the nature of the subject, technical content is unavoidable and can be found in the appendices.
>
> The first part of this document is a review of the fundamental concepts of reserve risk while the second part provides readers with a deeper understanding of the challenges faced within practical implementation of reserve risk. The authors hope that this publication will generate discussions in the general insurance actuarial community prompting the development of a common best practice approach to reserve risk. As such it is recommended that this document be circulated to all practitioners involved in reserving and reserve risk.

**Slides.** A taster of two topics from the paper. First, the disconnect between reserving and reserve risk: why the scaling issue arises (inconsistencies in data, model and parameters), why no scaling method is ideal, and the long-term remedy of building stochastic methods into reserving. Second, "how wrong is our estimation of wrong?": 1,000 triangles simulated from Mack's model with known parameters, comparing true CoV with Mack formula and bootstrap estimates.

The paper permits free distribution in its full original form; this copy is unaltered.
